Our Story

Built From
The Ground Up

STRIDE was born in a small workshop in 2013, where a single idea took shape: footwear should work harder for the people who wear it. Not louder, not flashier — just better.

Every material is sourced with intent. Every stitch is placed with precision. We partner with sustainable suppliers, test in real conditions, and refuse to ship anything we wouldn't wear ourselves.
